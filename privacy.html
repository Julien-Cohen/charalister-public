Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

In short: CharaLister does not collect, transmit, share or sell any of your data.

1. Data we collect

None. CharaLister has no account system, no sign-up and no server. We do not collect personal information, usage statistics, device identifiers or location.

2. Data you enter in the app

The books, series, characters, notes and relationships you create are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot access, read or recover them.

3. Analytics, advertising and tracking

CharaLister contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking technology of any kind.

4. Third parties

Because no data leaves your device, we do not share data with any third party.

5. Backups and deletion

Your data may be included in your device's own backup (for example, an iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings. This is managed by your operating system, not by CharaLister. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, permanently removes everything you stored in it.

6. Children

Since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at contact@example.com.
