Never lose track of a character again.

CharaLister is a simple companion app for readers. Note who is who, what you learn about them and when, and find it again in seconds.

Add characters

Save a first name and/or birth name, a comment, and the chapter where the character first appears.

Search instantly

Look up any character by first name or birth name and read everything you have noted about them.

Notes by chapter

Attach new information to a character along with the chapter or episode where you learned it.

Relationships

Link characters together on their profile to remember who is related to whom.

Books and series

Manage several books at once. For a series, chapters become episodes. Set a current book and a current chapter so new notes go in the right place.

Spoiler-aware

Because every note is tied to a chapter, you always know where a piece of information comes from.

Your data stays yours

CharaLister does not collect, send or sell your data. Everything you write stays on your device.
